## What is Health Equity and Climate?

Learn about the intersection of health equity and climate change — how environmental degradation and extreme weather disproportionately impact the health of marginalized populations.

## Why It Matters

Understanding health equity and climate is essential for organizations navigating the transition to a sustainable economy. As regulatory frameworks tighten and stakeholder expectations evolve, this concept plays an increasingly central role in corporate strategy, risk management, and reporting.

## Key Considerations

- **Regulatory landscape**: National and international frameworks are increasingly incorporating health equity and climate into mandatory disclosure and compliance requirements.
- **Stakeholder expectations**: Investors, customers, and communities expect organizations to demonstrate awareness and action.
- **Strategic opportunity**: Early movers who integrate health equity and climate into their strategy gain competitive advantage and build long-term resilience.
- **Measurement and reporting**: Robust metrics and transparent reporting are essential for accountability and credibility.

## The Path Forward

Health Equity and Climate is not a standalone issue — it intersects with broader sustainability challenges including climate resilience, social equity, and governance reform. Organizations that take a systems-level approach will be best positioned to navigate complexity and create lasting value.
