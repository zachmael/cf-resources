## What is Climate Finance?

Learn about climate finance — the flow of funds toward climate change mitigation and adaptation activities, from public institutions, private investors, and multilateral development banks.

## Why It Matters

Understanding climate finance is essential for organizations navigating the transition to a sustainable economy. As regulatory frameworks tighten and stakeholder expectations evolve, this concept plays an increasingly central role in corporate strategy, risk management, and reporting.

## Key Considerations

- **Regulatory landscape**: National and international frameworks are increasingly incorporating climate finance into mandatory disclosure and compliance requirements.
- **Stakeholder expectations**: Investors, customers, and communities expect organizations to demonstrate awareness and action.
- **Strategic opportunity**: Early movers who integrate climate finance into their strategy gain competitive advantage and build long-term resilience.
- **Measurement and reporting**: Robust metrics and transparent reporting are essential for accountability and credibility.

## The Path Forward

Climate Finance is not a standalone issue — it intersects with broader sustainability challenges including climate resilience, social equity, and governance reform. Organizations that take a systems-level approach will be best positioned to navigate complexity and create lasting value.
