## Overview

Compare ecosystem services and natural capital — two foundational concepts in environmental economics that frame how we value and account for nature's contributions to human well-being.

## Ecosystem Services

Ecosystem Services represents a distinct approach within the sustainability landscape. Understanding its scope, methodology, and applications is essential for organizations working to align with evolving frameworks and expectations.

### Key Features
- Specific focus and scope
- Established methodology and standards
- Growing adoption across sectors

## Natural Capital

Natural Capital offers a complementary perspective that addresses different dimensions of the same underlying challenge. Organizations often benefit from understanding both approaches.

### Key Features
- Distinct scope and application
- Evolving standards and frameworks
- Cross-sector relevance

## Key Differences

| Dimension | Ecosystem Services | Natural Capital |
|-----------|---|---|
| **Primary Focus** | Specific scope | Broader/different scope |
| **Framework** | Distinct standards | Alternative standards |
| **Application** | Targeted use cases | Complementary use cases |

## When to Use Each

The choice between Ecosystem Services and Natural Capital depends on your organization's specific needs, regulatory context, and strategic objectives. Many leading organizations integrate elements of both approaches into their sustainability strategy.

## The Bottom Line

Both Ecosystem Services and Natural Capital play important roles in advancing sustainability. Rather than choosing one over the other, consider how they complement each other and how your organization can leverage both to drive meaningful progress.
