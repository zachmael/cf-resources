## Overview

Compare the Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) with the CBD Global Biodiversity Framework — two complementary approaches to nature-related risk and conservation.

## TNFD

TNFD represents a distinct approach within the sustainability landscape. Understanding its scope, methodology, and applications is essential for organizations working to align with evolving frameworks and expectations.

### Key Features
- Specific focus and scope
- Established methodology and standards
- Growing adoption across sectors

## CBD Global Biodiversity Framework

CBD Global Biodiversity Framework offers a complementary perspective that addresses different dimensions of the same underlying challenge. Organizations often benefit from understanding both approaches.

### Key Features
- Distinct scope and application
- Evolving standards and frameworks
- Cross-sector relevance

## Key Differences

| Dimension | TNFD | CBD Global Biodiversity Framework |
|-----------|---|---|
| **Primary Focus** | Specific scope | Broader/different scope |
| **Framework** | Distinct standards | Alternative standards |
| **Application** | Targeted use cases | Complementary use cases |

## When to Use Each

The choice between TNFD and CBD Global Biodiversity Framework depends on your organization's specific needs, regulatory context, and strategic objectives. Many leading organizations integrate elements of both approaches into their sustainability strategy.

## The Bottom Line

Both TNFD and CBD Global Biodiversity Framework play important roles in advancing sustainability. Rather than choosing one over the other, consider how they complement each other and how your organization can leverage both to drive meaningful progress.
