## Sustainability Landscape

Explore El Paso's sustainability initiatives — from solar energy leadership and water conservation in an arid climate to binational environmental cooperation with Ciudad Juárez.

## Climate and Environmental Context

El Paso, TX faces unique environmental challenges shaped by its geography, climate, and economic base. Local government, businesses, and community organizations are working together to build climate resilience and advance sustainability goals.

## Key Initiatives

- **Climate action planning**: The city has developed or is developing comprehensive climate action plans with measurable targets.
- **Clean energy**: Investments in renewable energy and energy efficiency are accelerating across public and private sectors.
- **Water management**: Water conservation and infrastructure resilience are central to the city's sustainability strategy.
- **Environmental justice**: Ensuring equitable access to clean air, water, and green space for all communities.

## Opportunities for Businesses

Organizations operating in El Paso, TX have significant opportunities to lead on sustainability — from reducing operational emissions and improving supply chain resilience to engaging with local government on climate policy and workforce development.
